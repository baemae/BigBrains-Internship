Shipping: Orders are shipped within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 days within Pakistan and costs 200 rupees. Orders above 5,000 rupees get free shipping.

Returns: Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging. Refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.

Payment methods: We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery adds an extra fee of 100 rupees.

Order tracking: After your order ships, we send a tracking number by email and SMS. You can also check the status on the My Orders page.

Damaged items: If a product arrives damaged, send us a photo within 48 hours. We will replace it free of charge or give a full refund.

Warranty: Electronics come with a 1-year warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Water damage and accidental drops are not covered.

Account help: To reset your password, click Forgot Password on the login page. To delete your account, contact customer support.

Customer support: Live chat and email support is available from 10 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday. Support is closed on Sundays.

Discounts: Students get a 10 percent discount with a valid student ID card. New newsletter subscribers get 5 percent off their first order.

International shipping: We currently deliver only within Pakistan. International shipping is not available.
